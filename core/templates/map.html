How WorkCafe works

WorkCafe helps you find a cafe where you can actually get some work done - somewhere with decent wifi, a power outlet and a quiet corner, not just any coffee shop.

You can search cafes and look at the map without needing an account. If you sign up, you can see more cafes, register a new cafe you've found, and check in to the cafe you are currently working from - so you can see who else is checked in there right now.

When you book a spot, you can also see what the weather will be like on that day.
