How WorkCafe works

WorkCafe helps you find a cafe where you can actually get some work done - somewhere with decent wifi, a power outlet and a quiet corner, not just any coffee shop.

You can search cafes and look at the map without needing an account. If you sign up, you can also register a new cafe you've found and book a spot in advance, so you know you'll have somewhere to sit when you get there.
